Privacy Policy & Support

Last updated: October 10, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. fsotea has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. We do not operate any servers that receive your data, and the app makes no network connections to us.

Your study progress

The ratings you give each card (missed, partly, got it) are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

fsotea does not collect data from anyone, including children.

About the content

fsotea is an independent study aid for the Job Knowledge section of the Foreign Service Officer Test (2026). It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Department of State. The cards may contain mistakes.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Support & contact

Questions, problems, or corrections: reach us at rw427@cornell.edu.
